Rencana Perkuliahan UTS

Daftar Modul Materi (Pekan 1 - 7)

PEKAN 01 Siap Dipelajari

Introduction to Distributed Systems

Definisi model terpusat vs terdistribusi, 6 tujuan desain (transparansi, dependability, skalabilitas), klasifikasi sistem (Cluster vs Grid), dan 8 asumsi keliru jaringan.

Buka Modul Pekan 1 →
PEKAN 02 Siap Dipelajari

Architectures in Distributed Systems

Gaya arsitektur berlapis, RESTful vs SOAP, Temporal & Referential coupling, Linda tuple space, Chord Ring, Flooding vs Random Walk, dan konsensus Blockchain.

Buka Modul Pekan 2 →
PEKAN 03 Siap Dipelajari

Processes in Distributed Systems

Thread vs proses, context switching costs, user-level vs kernel-level threads, arsitektur dispatcher/worker, virtualisasi (VMM) & kontainer, serta migrasi kode.

Buka Modul Pekan 3 →
PEKAN 04 Siap Dipelajari

Communication

Protokol berlapis & middleware, transient vs persistent, synchronous vs asynchronous, RPC & stubs, message queues (MOM/ZeroMQ/AMQP), serta protokol gossip (anti-entropy).

Buka Modul Pekan 4 →
PEKAN 05 Siap Dipelajari

Coordination

Sinkronisasi jam fisik (NTP), Lamport Logical Clocks & Vector Clocks, algoritma Mutual Exclusion (Centralized, Ricart-Agrawala, Token Ring), serta algoritma pemilihan koordinator (Bully & Ring).

Buka Modul Pekan 5 →
PEKAN 06 Siap Dipelajari

Naming

Names, Identifiers, dan Addresses. Flat Naming (DHT Chord & forwarding pointers), Structured Naming (Domain Name System / DNS hierarki & iterasi), serta Attribute-Based Naming (Direktori LDAP).

Buka Modul Pekan 6 →
PEKAN 07 Siap Dipelajari

Consistency and Replication

Alasan replikasi & trade-off, Data-Centric consistency (Sequential, Causal, Eventual), Client-Centric consistency (Monotonic Reads/Writes, Read-Your-Writes), serta protokol Quorum-based.

Buka Modul Pekan 7 →
